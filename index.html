HOW TO PLAY

  1. AIM with your mouse and CLICK / TAP to shoot balloons.
  2. Only the GLOWING BALLOON can be popped — it holds your NEXT TASK.
  3. When a balloon pops, its task appears on the TASK STRIP. Go do it in real life!
  4. Come back and press ✓ DONE — only then does the next balloon unlock and start glowing.
  5. Pop balloons in order. Locked balloons will not burst!
  6. Finish all 8 tasks to hit TASK CLEAR!
